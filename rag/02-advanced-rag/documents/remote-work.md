# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation period are eligible to work
remotely. New hires are expected to work from the office during their first 90 days
to support onboarding and team integration.

## Hybrid Schedule
The default company policy is a hybrid model: employees are expected to be in the
office at least 2 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday designated as core
in-office collaboration days. The remaining days may be worked remotely.

## Fully Remote Requests
Employees who wish to work fully remotely must submit a request to their manager
and to HR, explaining the business justification. Fully remote arrangements are
reviewed on a case-by-case basis and are subject to role requirements.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time home office stipend of
$500 for employees working remotely more than 2 days per week. Requests for
additional equipment should go through the IT helpdesk.

## Working Hours While Remote
Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours, 10 AM to 4 PM
in their local time zone, regardless of their broader working schedule.

## International Remote Work
Working remotely from a country other than the employee's registered work location
requires prior approval from HR and Legal, due to tax and compliance implications.
Such requests must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance.
