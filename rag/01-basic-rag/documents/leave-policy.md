# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Unused leave can be carried
forward into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any leave beyond that cap is
forfeited unless approved by a manager for exceptional circumstances.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry
forward and resets on January 1st. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a
doctor's note is required before returning to work.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid leave. Parental leave must be taken
within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date.

## Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days
in advance, except for sick leave, which can be reported on the day of absence.
Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year, aligned with the local national
holiday calendar. Employees required to work on a public holiday are compensated
with a substitute day off within 30 days.
