# Contractor Policy

## Overview
This document covers policies specific to contractors and does not
duplicate content already covered in the Leave Policy, Remote Work
Policy, or Engineering Practices documents. Contractors should refer
to those documents for general company policy and to this document
for contractor-specific exceptions.

## Remote Work Eligibility for Contractors
Contractors are not subject to the standard hybrid schedule described
in the Remote Work Policy (2 days per week in-office). Contractors may
work fully remotely by default, provided this is agreed upon in their
contract. However, contractors engaged on-site for a specific project
requiring physical presence (e.g., hardware testing, secure facility
access) must follow the in-office schedule specified in their
Statement of Work.

## Leave and Time Off
Contractors are not entitled to the company's paid annual leave, sick
leave, or parental leave benefits described in the Leave Policy, as
contractors are not employees. Time off for contractors is governed
entirely by the terms of their individual contract or Statement of
Work. Contractors should coordinate planned absences directly with
their engagement manager.

## Equipment
Contractors are generally expected to use their own equipment unless
otherwise specified in their contract. The $500 home office stipend
described in the Remote Work Policy does not apply to contractors.

## Production Changes and Approval
Contractors are permitted to submit pull requests and participate in
code review under the same process described in Engineering Practices.
However, contractors are NOT permitted to approve pull requests that
touch authentication, billing, or data deletion logic. Those changes
still require two approvals as normal, but at least one approving
reviewer must be a full-time employee, regardless of how many
contractor approvals are also obtained.

## Contract Renewal and Offboarding
Contractor engagements are reviewed 30 days before their end date.
Access to company systems, including source control and production
credentials, must be revoked within 24 hours of a contractor's
engagement ending, whether the engagement ends on schedule or early.
